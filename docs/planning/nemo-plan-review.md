## Verdict

**Is the plan implementable as written?** No, not strictly as written: two defects prevent §18.1 acceptance — Appendix A has no per-decision entries, so the mandated change-control path (an Appendix A decision-log entry plus an ADR for ABI/object/target/IR changes, per §0.2 and §15.7) cannot be executed, and milestone gates G1/G2 are owned by M1/M2 while their evidence (KR16 descriptor/sysroot/backend, the linker and image writer, and KR16 test runs) lands at M3/M7. Substantively yes: the architecture, target matrix, corpus tiers, gates and language staging are coherent, every D-xxx cited in the body exists in §3, every cited sibling file exists, and Appendices C and D match §4 and §6 — once findings 1 and 2 are fixed the plan can be implemented.

**Are the §16 milestone gates achievable at the milestones that own them?** No as written: G2 is the gate of M2 (repeated at M3) but requires `hello.c` and `sum.c` to run on KR16, which does not exist until M7, and the KR32 half of G2 needs the linker and image writer that arrive at M3; G1's "on both targets" needs the KR16 data model/descriptor that M1 only provides as a skeleton (the KR16 descriptor is an M7 deliverable, §16.1 line 1332 and §9.6 lines 715-722). The remaining gates do line up with their owners: G0→M0, G3/G4→M4, G5→M5, G6→M6, G7→M7, G8→M8, G9→M10 (with G9 partial at M9).

**Do the body and the appendices tell the same story?** Mostly yes: Appendix C matches §4.1/§4.2 row for row, Appendix D matches §6.1/§6.2 staging row for row, and all 18 Appendix B compile-first files exist and fit §5.2's tiers. No in three places: Appendix A's L0 row contradicts the body's staging (variadic templates and multiple/virtual inheritance listed as the C++11 floor vs v1.1/v1.2 in §6.2, D-025, D-026 and Appendix D.2), Appendix A has no per-decision entries (only a collective "See §3" row), and its G1–G5 decision IDs collide with the §5.7 gate IDs G0–G9 with no G4 decision present. These are documentation and process contradictions, not substance.
